# Disclosure Policy

## How I report
- I send a private report to the vendor's security contact or VDP inbox.
- A report contains the root cause, reproduction steps, impact, and a fix suggestion.
- I ask for a response within a reasonable time and follow up once if there is none.

## Timelines
- I generally keep findings private for up to 90 days from the report date, unless the vendor asks for more time or confirms a fix earlier.
- If a vendor confirms a fix, I may publish after they have shipped it.
- If a vendor does not respond, I give notice before publishing.

## What I publish
- Only findings that are fixed, or past their disclosure window, with vendor identity anonymized unless the vendor agrees to be named.
- No working exploit code for unfixed issues.
- No customer data, credentials, or internal hostnames, ever.

## What I do not publish
- Anything still inside an active disclosure window.
- Details that would help someone exploit an unpatched system.
- Claims I cannot reproduce or that are not yet verified.
